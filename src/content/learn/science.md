---
title: The Science of Stretchy Cheese
description: Why mozzarella stretches and peels while other cheeses just crumble.
---

Mozzarella's protein structure is unique among cheeses. Heating and kneading the curd aligns
its casein proteins into parallel fibers, similar to strands of rope. This alignment is what
allows the cheese to be pulled apart into individual strings rather than breaking randomly.
